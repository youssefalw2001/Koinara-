# Al-Muraqib — The Observer

**Al-Muraqib** is the final product direction for this repo.

It is a mobile-first **Social Intelligence Suite** where users create radar links, post them on social platforms, collect observer signals, and turn their top observers into a **Crown Council**.

The product is designed for GCC / MENA social behavior: curiosity, crushes, exes, masked interest, repeat visitors, status, and story-link virality.

The simple pitch:

> Create your Social Radar. Find your Crown Council.

Arabic positioning:

> سوّي رادارك وشوف مين يدخل دائرتك.

---

## Final Product Decision

The main app is **Al-Muraqib**.

**Daira is no longer the main app.** Daira / Crown mechanics are now only the internal status game layer inside Al-Muraqib.

Final structure:

```text
Main app: Al-Muraqib — The Observer
Main product: Social Intelligence Suite
Main feature: Social Radar links
Emotional modes: Crush Radar, Unfinished Radar, Signal Check, General Radar
Money/status layer: Crown Council, paid boosts, masked mode, consent-based reveal requests
```

The app should not be positioned as hacking, spyware, stalking, or real identity decryption. It should be positioned as **social signals, masked observers, radar questions, repeat activity, and consent-based reveal mechanics**.

## One-Sentence Concept

**Al-Muraqib is a Social Intelligence Suite where users post a radar link, people enter as observers, answer a signal question, and the Top 3 observers compete for Crown Council status.**

---

## What Users Gain

A user creates a Social Radar link and posts it on Snapchat, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, or anywhere else.

When people click, the owner gets a dashboard showing:

- Observer Log
- Public or Masked observers
- Repeat visitor signals
- Interest Score
- Radar question answers
- Crown Council Top 3
- Boost activity
- Shareable Verdict cards

The emotional questions the product answers:

- Who is watching me?
- Who keeps coming back?
- Do they think about me?
- Is there unfinished business?
- Who wants to be noticed?
- Who is in my Top 3?

## Radar Modes

### 1. General Radar

For broad curiosity and social attention.

Example questions:

- Who is watching my story?

### 2. Crush Radar

For romantic curiosity.

- Are we friends or something more?

### 3. Unfinished Radar

For exes and unresolved tension.

Example questions:

- Would you reply if I texted?

### 4. Signal Check

For simple social feedback.

Example questions:

- What is my vibe to you?
- Do I seem mysterious?
- Would you want to know me better?

## Crown Council

Crown Council is the app's status and monetization layer.

The Top 3 observers on a radar become:

```text
#1 Crown King
#2 Diamond Prince
#3 Golden Guard
```

Observers can climb by:

- Entering the radar
- Answering the signal question
- Returning multiple times
- Sending boosts
- Choosing public or masked mode
- Sending a premium anonymous message later

The owner can share their Crown Council as a story card to create more traffic.

## Safety and Trust Rules

Al-Muraqib must stay inside safe social-gaming boundaries:

- No phone number selling
- No private Snapchat / WhatsApp rewards
- No adult content
- No meetups
- No minors
- No hacking claims
- No scraping social accounts
- No guaranteed identity reveal
- Reveal features must be consent-based
- Owner and observer should both have block/report controls

Positioning:

> Entertainment only. Signal-based social radar. Consent-based reveal mechanics.

## First-Month Revenue Logic

This model does not require a huge creator marketplace at launch.

It only needs people creating radar links and observers paying for status or privacy.

Realistic first-month ranges:

```text
Weak launch: $0-$50/day
Decent launch: $50-$300/day
Good first month: $300-$1,000/day
Strong local hit: $1,000-$5,000/day
Viral spikes: $5,000-$20,000/day
```

The first proof target:

> Can one radar link generate $20-$100 in paid boosts or masked-mode purchases in one day?
