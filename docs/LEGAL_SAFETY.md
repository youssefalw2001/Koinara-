# Legal-Safety Product Rules

Al-Muraqib should feel powerful, but it must stay consent-based.

## Allowed

- Track interactions that happen inside our own PWA.
- Ask visitors to submit a handle and platform.
- Capture basic browser/device metadata only after disclosure and consent.
- Use coarse country/region from infrastructure headers.
- Store hashed IP addresses with a secret salt.
- Show timing, repeat visits, heatmaps, and behavioral labels as estimates.
- Use identity hints only when the visitor consents and the poster has a paid unlock.

## Not allowed

- Do not claim to reveal Instagram/Snapchat/WhatsApp story viewers.
- Do not access official IG/Snap/WhatsApp private APIs.
- Do not scrape private data.
- Do not collect exact GPS location silently.
- Do not use canvas/audio/WebGL covert fingerprinting.
- Do not reveal contact identity without consent.
- Do not label people as stalkers or obsessed.

## Safer Product Language

Use:

- Social Radar
- Trust Graph
- High-interest signal
- Repeat observer
- Circle Pulse
- Consent-based identity hint

Avoid:

- Stalker detector
- Spy tool
- Close Friends hack
- Secret identity reveal
- Story viewer detector
